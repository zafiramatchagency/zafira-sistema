## What is Zafira?

<!-- nota: introducción (reemplaza la que tenga la sección; si no tiene, va como primer párrafo bajo el título). -->

An introduction agency with a matrimonial purpose: Zafira Limited Edition S.A.S., with its office in Bogotá. Gentlemen are men in the United States who contract a professional search and guidance service. You never pay anything and you do not receive money for taking part. It is not a dating app or a profile site: there is no list where anyone can see you.

<!-- nota: pilar 1. -->

**Verification on both sides**
Your identity, background and marital status are verified under Colombian law. Gentlemen go through a verification process before any introduction and before they can see your profile.

<!-- nota: pilar 2. -->

**Your profile is private**
It does not appear on any open page or social network. Only a gentleman who has passed his verification can learn about it, and only if you accept that introduction. None of your details are shared with a gentleman without your decision.

<!-- nota: pilar 3. -->

**Guidance**
An advisor guides you at every step, from the first interview. If both sides are interested, the in-person meeting is organised and accompanied by the Zafira team: you will never be alone.
