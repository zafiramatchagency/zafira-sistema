## Steps section

<!-- nota: el título de la sección ya se cambia a "Your path, in five steps" con W15. Subtítulo: reemplaza la versión inglesa de "Un proceso exclusivo diseñado para encontrar a la persona especial". -->

For gentlemen, always in this order: each payment comes only after an answer from us.

<!-- nota: los números 01 a 05 se quedan como están. -->

**01 · Application · Free**
About 4 minutes. You tell us who you are and what you are looking for. Submitting it triggers no charge.

**02 · Preliminary review · Free**
Our team reads it. If your profile is not a fit, we tell you then, and you have paid nothing.

**03 · Evaluation and verification · USD 400**
Interview, identity check against an official document, background and marital status. You pay it only when we tell you that your profile can move forward, and verification begins the same day the payment clears. It does not reserve a place, it is not credited toward the programme, and it is non-refundable once the evaluation has begun, except for any cancellation or withdrawal right granted by applicable law.

**04 · Approval**
Zafira decides whether you enter the programme. Paying for the evaluation is not the same as being approved. If you are not approved, we tell you, the process ends there and the USD 400 is not returned.

**05 · The programme · USD 13,000**
A single payment, only if you are approved and decide to continue, with a contract before any charge. It includes a personal search, the selection and verification of each candidate, private introductions only when both people agree, the private Cartagena gathering, organised by Zafira, and your matchmaker from approval to that gathering. Not included: flights to and from Cartagena, and personal expenses outside the official programme.

<!-- nota: cierre de la sección. -->

If everything moves forward, the full path is USD 13,400. There is no other charge.
